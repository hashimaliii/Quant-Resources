# 📉 Quant Development: Mastery Hub & Resources

Welcome! This repository serves as a centralized hub for my journey into **Quantitative Development**. It contains a curated collection of foundational textbooks and technical guides required to bridge the gap between financial theory and high-performance engineering.

---

## 📚 Curated Bookshelf

### 💻 Technical & Systems Engineering
* **Effective Modern C++** (Scott Meyers) - Mastering move semantics and smart pointers.
* **C++ High Performance** (Andrist & Sehr) - Optimization for low-latency systems.
* **C++ Concurrency in Action** (Anthony Williams) - Multi-threading for the university cluster.
* **Cracking the Coding Interview** (McDowell) - Data Structures and Algorithms foundation.

### 📊 Mathematical Finance & Theory
* **Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives** (John C. Hull) - The "Bible" of financial engineering.
* **Principles of Quantitative Development** (Manoj Thulasidas) - Designing financial software.
* **Trading & Exchanges** (Larry Harris) - Understanding how markets actually function.

### 🧠 Interview & Logic Preparation
* **A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews (Green Book)** - Probability & Brainteasers.
* **Heard on the Street** - Quantitative questions from Wall Street interviews.
* **FAQ of Quant Finance** - Quick-reference concepts for rapid-fire technical rounds.
